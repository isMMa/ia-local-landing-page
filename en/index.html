It doesn't take bad intent. It just takes being faster than searching the file. And even if you don't handle sensitive data, every one of those calls to an external model is billed per token — and that bill only grows.

data leakage

You don't know where the information ends up once your team feeds it into general-purpose AI tools. Names, case histories, contract clauses — it all leaves through the same door as any other prompt.

legal exposure

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 is already in force. If you handle health data, legal proceedings, or decisions that affect people, you likely have transparency or risk-classification obligations — whether you know it or not.

zero traceability

Without your own infrastructure, there's no record to show if an auditor, a client, or a regulator ever asks how AI was used in a specific case.

cost per use

This applies even if you have nothing to hide. An open model on your own server doesn't charge per token: the variable cost becomes a fixed one, and it drops the more you use it.

Four pieces, one system

We're not selling you a language model. We build the infrastructure around it: where it lives, how it's audited, and who keeps it running.

A

Your own infrastructure

Open models (Qwen and others, including quantized variants) running on your own hardware, inside the European Union. If you don't have infrastructure yet, we help you choose what fits best. If your data is sensitive, it stays home; if it isn't, you stop paying per token to a frontier model.

B

Logging, security and uptime

Every team and every client gets its own access key, with independent usage logs. Encrypted traffic, network segmentation and active monitoring — by default, not as an add-on.

D

Training for your team so they get the most out of the AI: how to use it, what to ask it, and how to work with it safely and productively.
